## Architectural Analysis: Threshold Mismatch vs Bug

This is primarily a **system contract inconsistency (dead-zone threshold conflict)** rather than an unhandled exception or coding bug:

1. **Dead Zone between Ingest and Rough Cut**:
   - `IngestDetector` configures `min_scene_len_sec = 0.8s` (`src/core/config.py:268`). It considers any shot $\ge 0.8\text{s}$ to be a valid, distinct scene.
   - `RoughCutCleaner` unconditionally enforces `min_take_duration_sec = 1.5s` (`src/core/config.py:422`, `src/modules/rough_cut_cleaner.py:539`).
   - Consequently, **any valid scene detected between 0.8s and 1.5s is guaranteed to be dropped 100% of the time**, even when completely defect-free.

2. **Total Clip Eviction on Short Footage**:
   - When users provide short clips (e.g., 3s stock clips, social clips, or rapid montage footage), splitting them into sub-shots $< 1.5\text{s}$ causes the entire clip to vanish from the project, even if the raw footage is 3.0 seconds long and pristine.

### Proposed Solutions for Architect Review (Do Not Apply - For Architect Decision)

- **Option A (Threshold Alignment)**:
  Align `min_take_duration_sec` with `min_scene_len_sec` (e.g. change default `min_take_duration_sec` to `0.8s` or `1.0s`, or set `min_take = min(self.config.min_take_duration_sec, self.config.min_scene_len_sec)` in `RoughCutCleaner._segments_from_analysis`).
- **Option B (Zero-Defect Fallback / Poka-Yoke Preservation)**:
  In `RoughCutCleaner._segments_from_analysis`: if an entire SceneCut had zero defects detected (`len(defect_spans) == 0`), but `scene.duration < min_take`, clamp to `min_scene_len_sec` (0.8s) instead of dropping, ensuring valid short camera shots are preserved.
- **Option C (Short-Clip Profile / Genre Override)**:
  In `PipelineConfig` or staged genre options, allow fast-paced / short-footage modes (e.g. social vertical, montage) to scale `min_take_duration_sec` down to `0.5s - 0.8s`.
